Records: Best-Known Functions

Best-known cryptographic Boolean functions for given parameters, with the work that first reported each result. Organised by objective — because the criteria trade off (Siegenthaler's bound, the nonlinearity–algebraic-immunity tension, …), "best" is only meaningful once the optimised property and the fixed constraints are specified.

Work in progress — corrections welcome. This is a best-effort community record and may be incomplete or out of date. If you know a stronger result, a missing entry, or a correction to a first attribution, please message me on LinkedIn. Entries without a link cite the publication of record; ★ marks an open target.

Maximum nonlinearity

For even $n$ the maximum over all functions is the bent value $2^{n-1}-2^{n/2-1}$. Balanced functions cannot be bent, so their record is lower.

Maximum nonlinearity for odd $n \ge 9$ is open (best-known values exceed the bent-concatenation bound).

Algebraic immunity

The maximum possible algebraic immunity is $\lceil n/2 \rceil$ (Meier–Pasalic–Carlet, 2004). The meaningful record is the best nonlinearity attainable together with optimal AI.

Multiplicative complexity ($n = 6$)

Every 6-variable Boolean function can be implemented with at most 6 AND gates; the multiplicative complexity of all 150,357 affine classes is known (Çalık, Sönmez Türan & Peralta, 2018).

★ Open targets

  • $n = 8$, balanced, NL = 116 and AI = 4. NL=116 balanced functions are known (Stănică–Maitra–Clark, 2004) and AI = 4 is the optimum, but no function is known to achieve both.
  • Maximum nonlinearity of functions for $n \ge 9$.
  • A complete affine-equivalence classification for $n = 7$.
